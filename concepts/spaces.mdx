---
title: "Spaces"
description: "Spaces group related Agents without becoming conversations themselves."
---

A **Space** organizes related Agents. It is a group in the sidebar, not a
conversation or an Agent of its own.

## Create and arrange a Space

1. Drag one Agent onto another Agent to create a Space containing both.
2. Drag another Agent onto a member of that Space to add it.
3. Expand or collapse the Space from the sidebar as needed.

There is no separate flow for creating an empty Space. A Space begins when you
group Agents together.
