**Connected apps** let an Agent work with supported services under your current
permissions. Examples include Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Slack, Notion, Linear,
Granola, Dropbox, Zoom, and other available connectors.

## Connect or select an app

- Open **Connected apps** from the sidebar to manage accounts.
- Use the `@` picker in the composer to select a connected account for the turn.
- You can also ask the Agent to use an advertised app. Turnstone can show the
  appropriate connection or access card in the conversation.

Connection, per-chat access, and approval for a change are separate boundaries.
Connecting an account does not give every Agent unlimited access, and selecting
an app does not bypass the current permission mode.
